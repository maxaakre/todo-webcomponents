# 02 — Does the two-pane Triage survive a phone screen?

Type: prototype
Status: open
Blocked by: 01
Map: ../map.md

## Question

Triage is the interaction that makes this a daily plan rather than a list. The predecessor chose **Variant C, the two-pane sorter**: unfinished work left, today's plan filling up right, three actions per Task.

Two panes side by side is a desktop idea. On a phone it has roughly 380px to work with.

- Does the two-pane framing survive, or does the phone need a different layout?
- The predecessor's reasoning was that **Today stays visible so decisions are made in context**. If the panes stack, that context is lost — does the decision still hold?
- Are the three per-Task actions still reachable with a thumb?

Judge it on the real deployed URL from ticket 01, on the real phone. Not an emulator, not a resized window.
